The rules, simply

What qualifies, and what doesn't

GMV decides your tier. The checklist decides if you are eligible to be paid it.

Counts Qualifies

  • 5+ new, original TikToks posted Nov 11 – Nov 30 (Pacific Time)
  • At least 2 of them posted Nov 26 – Nov 30
  • Both hashtags: #matrixholiday and #matrixblackfriday
  • At least 15 seconds long, with the product clearly on camera
  • An active shoppable link to a Matrix product or bundle
  • Calls out the current deal and its deadline (11.11, Black Friday or Cyber Monday)
  • Stays public and shoppable through Nov 30, with its Spark Code submitted

Doesn't count Not eligible

  • Duplicate, reposted or re-uploaded content
  • Videos under 15 seconds, or without the product on camera
  • Deleted or private videos
  • Videos without an active product link
  • Videos missing either hashtag
  • Videos posted outside Nov 11 – Nov 30 (they still earn GMV, but do not count toward your 5)
  • Cancelled, returned, refunded or fraudulent orders (no GMV)

GMV: what counts

Every sale Nov 11 – Nov 30 on all your Matrix videos counts, Phase 1 videos included. It is your combined total, not per video.

Who checks what

  • This board checks: post dates, both hashtags, product link, video length, GMV
  • The Matrix team confirms: deal callout, product on camera, public status, Spark Codes, original content, refunds
