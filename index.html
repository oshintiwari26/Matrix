How the winners were picked: creators with 3+ videos posted Sep 11 – Oct 18 are ranked by Video-attributed GMV (direct + indirect) from the TikTok Shop Video Performance exports (Sep 1–30 and Oct 1–18), de-duplicated by video ID. Each creator earns the highest tier reached ($300 → $75, $1,000 → $200, $3,000 → $500, $5,000 → $800); tier slots are filled top-down by GMV. Tier slots nobody reached stay unclaimed.

Prizes are confirmed after verification of qualifying videos (Grand Opening call-out and dates, shoppable link, Spark Code, videos live through Oct 18) and after cancelled, returned or refunded orders are removed from GMV.
